TVMaestro Companion is a guide and remote control for your own TVMaestro server. Connect to the same local network as your server.

What you need and what this app does

A running TVMaestro server is required. Enter its address below; this app opens the guide and controls provided by that server.

You can browse the program guide, choose a television, tune channels, and manage multiview and playback controls supported by that television. Playback happens on the television, not on this phone or tablet.

Channels and guide data come from sources you configure on your server. No channels, subscriptions, or video content are included with this app.

To connect to a different server, use Settings → Change server in the guide.
