# Privacy policy

TVMaestro does not collect, track, or share personal data.

## Television apps (Android TV and Apple TV)

Those apps store a few settings on the device: the control port, an optional auth token, and whether the guide has added or paired this device. Those values stay on the device.

The app listens on your local network so the guide you run can start and stop playback. Video is requested from the server the guide uses. That traffic stays on your network.

## Phone and tablet companion

The companion stores only the server host, port, and scheme you enter so it can reconnect. Those values stay on the device. The guide UI ships in the app. After you connect, the app calls your TVMaestro server’s API on your network. The phone and tablet do not play video.

This project has no account system and no analytics service.
