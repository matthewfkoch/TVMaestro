# TVMaestro

TVMaestro plays video from the TVMaestro guide on your home network.

Add this device in the guide. The guide starts and stops playback and can show up to nine streams at once. Volume and mute in the guide use the same controls as the Siri Remote. Pair the Apple TV in the guide first. The television follows when Control TVs and Receivers is on.

The app does not include its own lineup. Leave it open, or pair it once in the guide so playback can start when the app is not in front. A paired guide can wake the Apple TV. The television turns on only if Control TVs and Receivers is enabled on the Apple TV.
