TVMaestro does not collect, track, or share personal data.

The app stores a few settings on the device: the control port, an optional auth token, and whether the guide has added or paired this device. Those values stay on the device.

The app listens on your local network so the guide you run can start and stop playback. Video is requested from the server the guide uses. That traffic stays on your network. This app has no account system and no analytics service.
