# Privacy policy

TVMaestro for Apple TV does not collect, track, or share personal data.

The app stores a few settings on the Apple TV itself: the control port, an optional auth token, and whether the TVMaestro guide has added or paired this device. Those values stay on the device.

The app listens on your local network so the TVMaestro guide you run can start and stop playback. Video is requested from the Channels DVR server you configure. That traffic stays on your network. This app has no account system and no analytics service.

Questions and source: [github.com/matthewfkoch/TVMaestro](https://github.com/matthewfkoch/TVMaestro).
