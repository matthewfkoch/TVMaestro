TVMaestro is a phone and tablet guide for a TVMaestro server on your home network. Open the app, enter the server address, and browse the same channel lineup and schedule you see in the browser guide.

Use the guide to start and stop playback on a paired television on your network. The phone and tablet do not play the streams. Playback stays on the television apps.

There is no account. The app stores only the server address you enter. It does not collect personal data and has no analytics service.

You can open a sample guide in the app without a server. Connect to your own server when you are ready to control a television.
